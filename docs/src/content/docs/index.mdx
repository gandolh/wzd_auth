Ward is the estate's identity service. It replaces six separately-designed auth
stacks with **one identity, one credential store, and one set of access
grants** — a Fastify + SQLite service at `/ward-api`, and the one login page in
the estate at `/ward`.

<dl class="ward-facts">
  <dt>Runs at</dt>
  <dd><code>https://gandolh.ro/ward</code> (UI) and <code>/ward-api</code> (API), one origin, no subdomains</dd>
  <dt>Owns</dt>
  <dd>Credentials, account identity, and <code>(subject, app, roles)</code> grants</dd>
  <dt>Does not own</dt>
  <dd>Anything an app stores about a person — apps keep their own rows, keyed on an opaque <strong>subject</strong></dd>
  <dt>Consumed by</dt>
  <dd>atrium · public-resource-map · newspapper · imbatranimOS · sports-app</dd>
  <dt>Signs with</dt>
  <dd>One Ed25519 key, held by Ward alone; apps get the public half over JWKS</dd>
</dl>

## The shape in a paragraph

A person lands on an app, gets bounced to `/ward/login?next=…`, and signs in
once. Ward writes a session and sets two cookies: a **15-minute EdDSA access
token** at `Path=/`, and a **30-day opaque refresh row** scoped to
`/ward-api/refresh`. Because every project is a sub-path on one origin, that
access cookie is already attached to the next request the person makes to any
app — no redirect dance, no CORS, no token relay. The app verifies the
signature locally, then asks Ward whether the session is still live and what it
may do, and caches that answer for 30 seconds.

## What this site is made of

Three sources, kept apart on purpose, because they go stale in different ways.

- **Authored** — the pages above. Written by hand against the code, and the
  place where architecture, the HTTP surface and the schema are described. The
  corpus has long recorded that it had no `architecture.md`, `api.md` or
  `data.md`; these are those pages.
- **Rendered from the corpus** — everything under *Deep dive* and *Status*. Those
  are generated on every build from `corpus/`, which is the project's own
  decision record. They carry a banner saying so. Edit the corpus, not the copy.
- **Generated from the code** — [`@ward/client`](/reference/client/) is TypeDoc
  over the package's public barrel, so it cannot drift from what the module
  actually exports. The diagrams are compiled by
  [archify](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify) from typed JSON in `docs/diagrams/`
  and validated before they ship.

:::note[One honest caveat, carried over from the corpus]
At the time of writing, **nothing here has ever run against a deployed Ward**.
Every verification is `app.inject`, a local socket, or a dev server that
*imitates* Caddy. The cookie paths are the part most likely to be wrong in a
way no local test can show, because `handle_path` strips a prefix that nothing
local strips. See [Status](/wiki/status/).
:::
